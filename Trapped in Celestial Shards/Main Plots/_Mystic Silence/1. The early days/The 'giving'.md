A mother grieving of sorrow
Fled to the sea

Wanting to drown in the waters blue
Yet, there was a stop

Looking up at the pretty moon
So bright in the night

It almost felt warm
As she made a wish so dear

"Please, bring me a child."

Asking, as the water went to her knee.

"Please."

Begging, as the water went past her torso.

"That's all I need."

Glistening eyes looking up at the pretty moon.


## ***A wave swallowed her.***


And, when she came too it, in her arms laid an egg.
Big, and slightly soft, she handled it with care.

"Thank you." She whispered.
