She cared for the egg
As if it was the only thing worth living for

People didn't understand
They couldn't see

Her priorities seemed so wrong to them all
And soon, no one bothered asking if she wanted to come along
To join in with their fun

"Will the egg be safe?"
"Can I take the egg with me?"

They didn't want to deal with those questions
Especially is it just lead her to sitting in the corner of their fun

So lonely and slow her life began to feel
But, she had the egg, her one saving grace

Then.

A lovely day.

A knock on the door.

A person with a friendly face, telling of a place where she can be safe and happy. Where everyone cares for one another and all is good. All is calm. They'll even help with accommodations, no need to worry about worldly things.

She smiled.

Accepted.


`----------------------------`


It all went smoothly.
And soon she was where she was meant to be.

And, in that place.
She heard of revery.

Looking down at the egg.
The most special of eggs.



The place, the compound, her home to be.
It held a peculiar belief, that the celestials in the sky could help you find your way.

"The stars are always watching, they've seen it all before, they can help you find the right path for you." The friendly people would say.

"And what of the moon?" She'd ask, holding her egg.

"The moon is a kindness too, for she can guide you through the night with her pretty light."

"I think she gave me my child. I think she granted my wish."

"Oh?"

"Yes." Looking at her beloved egg with adoration.

"Ah, I see, you too must have been blessed then."

"Blessed?"

"Well, of course! Our dear founder has a celestial child too."

"They do?" 

"Yes. A pure child of the stars, a true angle."

"Oh."

`----------------------------`

Then, one day, the egg finally hatched.

A little larvae, reminiscent of fluffy dog with scales came through.
