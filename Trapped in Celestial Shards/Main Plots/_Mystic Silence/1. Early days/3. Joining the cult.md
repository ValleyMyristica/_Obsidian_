
The dishes are being cleaned while moms beloved baba is rolling around on the living room carpet. Right where she can see. 

*Knock. knock.*

There is a knock at the door. 

She stops. Putting the plate she just cleaned to the side, slowly. Turning to keep an eye on her sweet child. Moving, drying her hands as she approaches.

"Sweetie?" She softly speaks.

Her sweet kid looks at her.

"I'll be just a moment." 

The kids head tilts in confusion.

Unsure, she gives a smile back as another knock is heard. Should really get to that, it's no good to have such loud noises around such a sweet child.

Moving towards the door, safety secure, making sure the door can't be opened more than half her own face. She peaks out. 

"Ah! Hello!" A person starts in a chipper voice. "I'm here to help."

"Help?" She questions, the tension in her shoulders letting go slightly.

"Yes! With how the world has started to become, with all it's uncertainty and danger, I'm here to help you find a place of safety, where you can live comfortably without all these worries."

"Really?" Interest peeked.

"Indeed." A nod. "People always wait for a sign, maybe this is yours?" Handing over a little pamphlet. 

Taking it.

"You're very welcome to simply come by and watch us, get a feel for things. We even have a free test you can do to help you figure out more of what you need in life. Nothing wrong in giving it a try, right?"

"I guess."

"Wonderful! There will be a talk at lunch tomorrow, if you're able, it would be wonderful to see you."

"What's the talk about?"

"How to find your way in life, there will also be sandwiches for anyone who comes, so it's a nice break to a day, sitting and listening, wouldn't you say?"

"Well, that *does* sound nice, but..."

"Can I count on you?"

"Huh? I..." Not a big fan of leaving the house.

"If you **do** come, I'll make sure to make you a special sandwich, all for you, are you allergic towards anything?"

"No... not particularly, though, not a big fan of fish."

"Okay! I'll be sure to make a lovely sandwich for you with nothing fish related, do you like curry?"

"Curry is nice."

"Yay! It sure is! I'll make you the most wonderful curry sandwich. Is there anything else you'd like? I can give you more information is you need, though, most should be in the pamphlet."

"I..." Examining the paper in hand. "Thank you..." A quick glance towards the living room and back.. "Do you have... anything for... a child?"

"I can make your child a sandwich too. Any requests?"

"Just... something easy to chew?"

"I'm sure I can get that sorted for you, miss..?"

"█████"

"Lovely, and when you come, you can tell them you're looking for ███, that's me." Giving a little laugh.

"Okay, ███, I'll be sure to remember that."

"I'll remember you to miss █████, I even made sure to write everything down."

Smiling lightly.

"I really look forward to meeting you tomorrow!"

"Likewise."
