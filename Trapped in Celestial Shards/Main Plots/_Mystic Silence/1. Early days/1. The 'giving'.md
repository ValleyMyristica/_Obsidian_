A [[2. Characters/Side Characters/MS MOON/Mama|mother]], consumed by sorrow
Fled to the sea

Wanting to drown in the waters blue
For, what else is she meant to do?
A step-

Stops.

Looking up at the moon
So bright. A lonely night.
Shimmers dance like a flowing veil over the waters soft waves. Like fairies, out for a flight. On this lonely, lonely night. Yet, it feels warm, inviting. *The sea will welcome her.* Take her with all her failures. All her faults. All her... lonely.

Arms held close and a step towards the moon.

She makes a wish.

"Please, bring me a child."

Asking, water at her knees.

"Please."

Begging, water past her torso.

"That's all I need."

Glistening eyes looking up at the *kind* moon.


## ***A wave swallowed her.***


And, when she came to, in her arms laid an egg.
Big, and slightly soft, she handled it with care.
Feeling the texture, as if a knife to rip it apart like cloth.
Like it would be an easy meal for the world to consume.

It needs protection.
It needs a home.
It needs safety.

She can do that.
She'll do it better this time.

"Thank you." She whispered.
