A [[2. Characters/Side Characters/MS MOON/Mama|mother]].
Consumed by sorrow
Fled to the sea

Seeking the waters
Atonement and grace
Wanting to find her place.

Gaze towards the moon.
So bright. In a lonely night.
Shimmers on waves that dance like a flowing veil. 
Like fairies, out for a flight. Under the moons shine. 
As if there is still time.

The winds feel cold. 
The sight feels warm.
Inviting. *The sea will welcome her.* 
Take her with all her failures. All her faults. All her... lonely.


Arms held close and a step towards the moon.
She makes a wish for the one that was taken too soon.

"Please, bring me a child."

Walking, with the water at her knees.

"Please."

Begging, with the water past her torso.

"That's all I need."

Glistening eyes looking up at the *kind* moon.


## ***A wave swallowed her.***


And, when she came to, in her arms laid an egg.
Big, and slightly soft, she handled it with care.
Feeling the texture, as if a knife to rip it apart like cloth.
Like it would be an easy meal for the world to consume.

It needs protection.
It needs a home.
It needs safety.

She can do that.
She'll do it better this time.

"Thank you." She whispered.
