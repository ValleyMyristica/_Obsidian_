Halting, the sheep slowly moved towards the forest, instinct telling to keep away. 

Go far. 

*Then* die.

Laying down in the soft moss. Birds singing lullabies. The wind swaying the leaf's that shine so green in the suns warm light. Waiting there. Relaxing. Strength slowly leaving, as limpness takes over. Like salt dissolving in water.

Eyes close.

One final breath.

*Itching.*

Nose is itching.

---

A *sheep* makes it way out of the forest.
Makes its way to town.

The lively town with so many lively *bodies*.
