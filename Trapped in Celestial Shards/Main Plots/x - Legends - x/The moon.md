Moons so high
Two by two

Both loved for the soft light they shared
Always asked, “please come down here.”

Then one pretty night  
Both moons were full

One showed its truth  
As cracks started to form
A dragon broke through
And fell to the sea

Leaving one lonely moon up in the sky

The lonely moon
Made the waves of the sea sway

Letting the dragon rest softly in the water  
Under the lonely moons sweet light
