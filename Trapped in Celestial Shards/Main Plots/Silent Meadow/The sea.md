**Characters**
	[[Mystic]] and [[Saffron]]

**Background**
	 Saffron is finally warming up to the idea of bringing Mystic back to reality. Mystic and all other who lay in their mind. For now, he's doing his best to warm them up to what may be found outside. Mostly showing them around town, places one can eat, the park.
	 ㅤ
	 Though, today he'll just show the sea.
	 Give them something nice to look forward to.

---

*Reverent* guided by Saffron to a view he wanted them to see. 
Just another thing to help prepare for reality. 

"I'm sure you'll find it a lovely view." Saffron explains.

"If you say so." Not fully understanding what could be so interesting.

He smiles.

The two make their way.

The waves are softly rowing onto the rocky shore. 
A cold breeze combs through their hair.

"We're here." Saffron presents, guiding their eye towards the pretty view.

Twilight. Sky full of colour.
The sun glimmering on the water waves as it slowly starts to rise.

"What do you think?" He asks, turning to- "Reverent?" Are they... 

A soft shake of their head as eyes are transfixed on the view.
Watching it. Taking it all in. It's... pretty. So pretty. 
Like a memory they've missed. 
Something forgotten finally found.
Like a warm hug from mom.

A tear falls.

Then another.

And soon, all of them.
Falling to their knees, still transfixed on the view.

"...Mystic?"

Their head moves slightly towards the voice, but... they can't look away. Their eyes stay, watching the light sparkle like gold on the water. Mesmerizing.

"Do... you like it?" Unsure.

Stuck, they just give a hum of an acknowledgment in a tiny little nod.

"That's... nice..." Looking between them and the sea, he decides to sit down at their side. Joining,  appreciating the view. "I think it's pretty too." He comments.

"..pretty..." They repeat, as a quiet whisper.





After a long while, they turn to him, tears still fresh.

"I wanna go home."

He averts his eyes.

"I miss mom."

He turns his whole head away.

"I miss it." Cowering into themself.

"Okay." He acknowledges, dismissive. "What do you expect me to do about it?" Annoyed.


Opening to speak. Yet, so hard to say, what *can* they say? So... they give up. It's... useless... "I'm... sorry..." Feeling so *pathetic*.

"Don't. Don't say things you don't mean." He gets up.

"Where are you-"

"Away. Enjoy your view."

"Mmm." Still, they do as told.

Alone.

All alone.

With the pretties view, one they never knew.

The only thing that could make it prettier would be if it was the moon reflected in the water. Maybe some other night? Maybe... if this was real...

"I wish it was real."
