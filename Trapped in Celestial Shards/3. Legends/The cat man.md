My feline friends, stay inside
My lovely children, sleep at night
My hated humans, look outside

For in the dim light at night,
you might find a feral sight.

Beware of the cat man,
who stalks you at night

Beware of the cat man,
who follows out of sight

If you run, he will catch you
If you hide, he will find you
If you screech, he will **kill** you

For he can listen
He can run
And
He can cut off your tongue

Don’t leave the house
Don’t open the door
Don’t follow the man

You can’t be sure
Is he friend?
Is he foe?

I hope, we’ll never know


> There is danger in [[1. Places/Island of the lost/The forest|The forest]] and this legend is to highlight that
