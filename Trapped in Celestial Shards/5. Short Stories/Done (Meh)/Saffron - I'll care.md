He was happy there.

You know?

He really was.

Playing with his sibling, so much fun they had. He even remembers how things were in the very beginning. Blooming a bit early, nothing *anyone* expected. Yet, there he was, flesh and blood, well... as much flesh you can expect of a plant at least.

All three looked at him with awe.

He started out as such a pretty flower.

"It looks like Saffron!" His sibling would say.

And, the ones considered parents agreed in a way.

Mother and father also said it would take a while until he'd be able to take a more mobile form. "He needs to get used to his magic." "But don't worry, when you're older you two can play together!" "It'll take around year, if you're lucky." 

That was the first lie he ever heard.

**It took a week.**

His sibling was visiting again.

*Crying.*

The parents were out for a meal, and the guard guarding the temple had learned to turn a blind eye towards the little human child visiting. 

The child slowly moved towards the alter where he had bloomed, calling out to those parents. But, all that returned was a echo.

Moving closer to the plant, eyes still vet with tears a throat feeling like a gargled mess. Slimy wet hands touched the alter.

"I-" The kid tried to speak, but coughing took the place of words.

It was... sad...

But, what can a flower do?

"I think my friends-" Gasping for air. "Hate me."

Slowly, the kid climbs up.

"They ignored me all day."

Drying the tears as they sit by the flower.

"I don't know what I did wrong?"

Glancing at the flower.

"I just..."

Then, hiding in their own legs.

"I'm annoying."

The flower can only listen as tears slowly seep into the soil.

"I just-" Hand trying to dry off the whole face. "I wish someone cared about me." Putting down the slimy wet hand by the flower to dry it off in the dirt. "I wish..." 

Words stop as tears take over and they retreat back into themselfs.

He wants to help.

To reach out a hand.

To tell them-

He gives them a hug.

And, they can feel *somebody*, a little tinier than themself, giving a hug.

"Huh?" Lifting their head to see hair the same pretty colour as the flower they're sitting beside.

"I'll... care-" Trying to convey feeling with a mouth so foreign feels... *weird*...


It's not really a *warm* hug.
But it is a comfort.
