Alone. Like normal. Playing in the sand. 

Building a little castle. Imagining a story, a fairy tale. 

*She's stuck in the castle, she never knew any other place to be. Only following along, that was her reality. **Then**. One day, a dragon came and stole the princess! The kingdom was SO worried! Cause everyone cared for the princess. Everyone loved the princess. She was loved by everyone.*

"Everyone wanted to save the princess." They animatedly tell their plushie. "So they did!" Happily telling the field mouse how, "the knights all went out searching." Drawing in the sand showing where all the knights went. Imagining how hard they all fought o find the princess.

"And then!" Drawing a line to a little hill of sand they've made. "One of the knights went to the **biggest** most **dangerous** mountain." Building up the hill. Making it more sturdy, even making a little cave close to the top. (They're the best at playing in the sand.)

"And there is where the knight found the princess."

They draw the knight to, *accidentally* making him look like the one person that seems to care.

"Cause this knight wouldn't give up. No matter what."

Drawing the princess in the sand, *accidentally* adding some *unique* features. *Features they have*. 

"He cares about her."

Poking the sand.

Looking at the picture that they drew.

Drawing a little heart around the two.

Looking.

A little smile emerging.
