
Sitting. Starting at the clock on the wall as it keeps ticking by.

Could play a game, or watch something dumb, or scroll and scroll till the mindless midnight hits. Could look out the window, listen to the rain and see how the clouds won't let in a single drop of sunshine, just rain, pouring. It his the window, tapping on the glass, as if it wants inside.

It's fine.

She's used to being alone.

Looking at her hands, looking at the stupid cut she got from being an *idiot* and wanting to climb a tree. *If mom finds out...*

Mom **won't** find out.

Examining the skin, it hurts at every touch. Holding things hurts.

Time is closing in.

*It'll be okay.*

Watching the time, ticking by.
Watching till a buzzing can be heard.
Watching while the door automatically opens.

"Dinner is ready." A robotic voice tells simply from the drone currently hovering in her room, waiting for it's next task to be triggered.

"Thank you." Moving towards the hall.
