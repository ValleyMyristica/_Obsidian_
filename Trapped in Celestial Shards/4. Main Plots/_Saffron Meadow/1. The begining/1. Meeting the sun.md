 Sitting by the table, back straight. Proper posture. In front, glistening porkchops and steamed vegetables. *It's good for you.* Everyone says. Only approved meals are allowed for consumption. 
 Approved meals that the 'assistance' are programed to make. It's always the same, every ingredient included. Except.

Clenching a fist around the fork.

*Mom **loves** me.*

Taking some food.

If she didn't, would the food really be made this precious?

"Are you *trying* to desecrate your food." Mom *asks*.

"..." Stopping all movement. The nice presentation of the plate, ruined. 

"You're old enough. You should know better." 

*I know*. Feeling the disappointment spread like mold under her skin. She's ten, she **should** know better. Mother is right. Always right.

"Alice."

"I'm sorry." The kid, Alice, is quick to respond.

"I don't need your 'sorry' I *need* you to do better." Mom states clearly. *She just wants the best for me.* "Is *this* how you eat in school?" Clearly, mom is just looking out for her child.

"I eat carefully in school."

"So it's simply at home you like to make a mess?"

"I..."

A sigh. "Have I not told you." *Mom's not happy.* "How important it is to always do your best, even if no one is looking?"

"I'm- I'll do better."

"Do your best."

"I'll do my best."

"And don't fail me."

"I promise."

"Good, now, don't forget your **metformin**." Mom reminds.

"Yes, mom." With a silent nod she takes the medicine that had so graciously been placed on the table. The 'assistants' had brought it along with the food. Currently they charging, as to not disturb the nice quiet of mealtime, a contrast to most of the time where they move around the house making sure everything is as it should. **Clean** and pristine. Mom really likes the deafening white, with a shimmering of gold.

That was all.

A quiet fell.

Eating in silence.

That is the norm.

It's a nice meal, well prepared, and... it's not every day you're allowed to eat with mom. She's a very busy woman, it's clear she cares. Why else would she be here having a nice meal together? It's... the only reason *has* to be...

"Alice?"

"Yes?" Willing to listen to whatever mom might say.

"I'll have to leave, I trust that you'll make sure all is nice when I get back?"

"Of course!"

"Good." Putting on her pretty glovers and leaving.

"I'm trusting you now. Okay?"

"Okay." Bringing forth a bright smile. Ignoring the cold terror creeping under her skin like black veins trying to seep into her blood. *It's fine.* She's fine, it's only good if mom trust her. Mom isn't scary. *Mom loves me.* This is a good thing.
