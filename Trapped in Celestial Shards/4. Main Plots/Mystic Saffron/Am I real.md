Sitting in their comfortable home.
Looking at their small little paws.

*They weren't like that before*

"Am I real?" The winter flakes sounding of words fall out, slowly resting in the quiet room. 

"Yes." A well-known voice echoes.

"Am I?" Softly moving the paws through the dust swimming in the air.

"Mystic. You're real." The voice sounds so distant.

"Mystic?" Where did that name even come from. "Who are you?"

"You're nice." The dust shifts. 
"You're kind." A paw is taken. 
"And." It's a soft touch. 
"You're **real**."

"How can I be real?" They ask of void eyes.

"How do you want me to answer that?" The face looks kind, pretty void eyes.

"Honest?"

"You're real to me." A soft smile lights, highlighting the pretty purple surrounding.

"What if I'm just a figment? A fractured shard of imagination?" Looking away from the pretty purple hair, up towards the wooden ceiling.

"Mystic."

"Yeah." Looking back, seeing him for all he is. **There.** Alive and breathing.

"You're real." Rubbing his thumb over the hand he's holding. "This is real." 

"How can you be sure?" Mystic pulls away.

"I can't." Blunt. "But." He stands up. "I think it's real enough." He gives them a snickering smile, one they're so happy to see. "Don't you agree?"

"Heh." Mimicking the smile. "I guess." Trying a little, despite how heavy the dust feels.


.
.
.

"I'm sorry." His smile falters.

"Huh?"

They can see his eyes fill with pity and sorrow.

*Their state is all his fault.*

"It's... okay..."

His tired eyes look at them.

They open their arms.

He falls into the hug, holding them softly.

A quiet moment.

"How I wish you didn't lie so much all the time." 

But it's only a moment.

Hugging them tighter. "I'm..." If only he hadn't done it. "I care about you." He wants to fix it. "I'm here for you." Or... fix what he *can* fix. 

They simply hug him more, hiding in his embrace.
