"You don't love me." Holding onto themself. "You said it yourself." Turning away. "So, you can leave." Looking at him. Begging. "Please."

They close their eyes. "Please." 

"I..."

"Why do you want me to leave?"

"*Do* you want me to leave?"

They curl up, like a ball on the sofa.

"Maybe it would be better if-"

"No." Claws digging into their furry arms. "You always say you don't like me." Holding their voice. "So..." Taking a breath to keep voice steady. "You can leave."

"Mystic..."

Quiet.

"I don't mind leaving." Taking a step towards them. "If that's what *you* want."

Head hiding in knees.

"*Is* it what you want?" Sitting down on the sofa, a few steps away.

Quiet.

Waiting.

Quiet.

Waiting.

Waiting.
