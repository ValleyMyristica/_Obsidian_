He's back.

He wakes.

Again and again.

He dreams of returning. A dream, so sweet and nice. A walk through the sparkling spring leaf's. A nightmare, a dance with death. A waltz in which one wrong move means a stab through the heart. Piercing and clear, an aching fear, with no escape. 

Trying to go through days as if it doesn't bother him.

Yet.

The bed terrifies.
And the night brings fear.

Surrounded by empty coffee mugs, in a room he's sure to loose.

"We're still looking for the man."

Watching something stupid to get his mind off things.

"Jonathan-"

Is still *missing*.

"Even with the help of volunteers, there have been no new developments."

What? They haven't found that stupid cave yet?

"If you have any information that might help locate him. Please contact-"

Maybe it would be worth telling them about the location?

"It will be rewarded."

And, what is there to lose?

"Again, please contact-"

Yeah. Yeah.

Taking his phone, he gives a call. A simple call, telling of the cave, suggesting that the *random* man might have taken some shelter there. To which they thank him and promise to look into it.

**Still missing.**

It says.

*Are they idiots?*

He thinks.

Calling again, asking. 
They can't give him that information.

He curses them.

They hang up.

*Idiots.*

*All of them.*

How can they not find a stupid cave?

*Fine.*

In frustration, he gets a map, locating where he likely was. Oh, wouldn't you know it, there *is* a stupid mountain there. Of course there is. Where else would the stupid legend come from? Or what? Did the fool just make all of that up?

Hmm...

Not that he's interested or wants to get back or anything, but...

With how much coverage the story has been getting, and that talk of a reward, *well*. He would be quiet the fool if he *didn't* try and 'seize the moment' or whatever generic motivation jargon people like to say. 

Not alone though, **never** alone. 

If the people can't find something as simple as a cave, then he'll just have to *show* them. How? With the help of a camera crew, of course. He'll just call a news station, say he has a possible idea, found a cave some time back, something worth looking into.

Then. He'll make them promise to reward him **handsomely** for all the views he's sure to bring in. Everyone will look like a fool for not finding that cave beforehand. All those people, and not a *single* one has found it, except **him**. Imagine the headlines!

So. He calls. And, despite their *annoying* reluctance, they agree.  It's thankful that things can be so easy when you know what to say, like your last name. *Ah, yes, he's my father.* HAH!
