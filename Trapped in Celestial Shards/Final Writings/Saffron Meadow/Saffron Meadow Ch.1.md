
The dining room is a pretty sight, walls adorned with conversations for the ages. Mementos of trips the average would never experience. Wonders most never get to see, let alone touch. And, in the center of it all, an ivory dining table, with a new flower arrangement at its center.

**Dinner is served.**

It's a pretty flower arrangement, with white hyacinth at its center. 

"Are you *trying* to desecrate your food?" A question from the right side of the arrangement, the side adorn with yellow carnations.

A nod, an answer from the left side, the one with yellow chrysanthemum.

"You're old enough. You should know better." 

"I know." 

"And yet." Disappointment.

"I'm sorry."

"I don't need your 'sorry' I *need* you to do better. Is *this* how you eat in school?"

"I eat carefully in school."

"So it's simply at home you like to make a mess?"

"I..." It was just really good food today...

A sigh. "Well, if you can't treat your food right, maybe you shouldn't have any food at all?"

"I'll do better." Head low. Meek little kid, no older than 10, wanting to be better, but it ends up only being pretend.

"Then make sure to hold your fork proper, and knife like this." Showing.

"Okay." Copying, as she's done so many times before.

"I know you are capable Anika."

"Thank you mother."

"Which is why it makes me so sad when you fail to live up to your potential."

Listening. Slowly eating. Careful. Putting down the cutlery properly and reaching to take a sip of the refreshing beverage in front.
