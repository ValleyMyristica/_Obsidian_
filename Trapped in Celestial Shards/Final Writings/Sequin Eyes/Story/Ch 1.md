Glistening snow. Sparkling stars. A pretty night with the moon so bright.

"It amazes me, you know."

But the moon is hidden from view.

"I have no expectations for you, and yet."

Maybe it's only the stars that can be bright?

"You're able to disappoint."

"I'm sorry."

"I told you to be quiet."

"I'm..." Head low.

"And somehow, despite your big ears, you **can't** listen." 

Sinking down into themselves even more.

A deep sigh, one of disappointment, one of repeating facts that never change. Annoyed and unable to change it. "All I need from you is to stand there, in the background, doing *nothing*. How can that be so hard?"

"My legs started hurting..." A whisper. A whimper.

"Maybe you shouldn't lay down so much?"

"..."

"If you're unable to do something, just learn how to do it."

"I can't..."

"I know." Annoyance.
