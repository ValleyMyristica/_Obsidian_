He's back.

He wakes.

Again and again.

He dreams of returning. A dream, so sweet and nice. A walk through the sparkling spring leaf's. A nightmare, a dance with death. A waltz in which one wrong move means a stab through the heart. Piercing and clear, an aching fear, **no escape**. 

Going through days as if it doesn't bother him.

Yet.

The bed terrifies.
And the night brings fear.

Surrounded by empty coffee mugs, in a room he's sure to loose.

"We're still looking for the man."

Watching something stupid to get his mind off things.

"My brother, Jonathan he-"

Is still *missing*.

Well. They haven't found the cave. *That terrifying thing*. A sight he sees so clear when eyes are closed. He keeps them open. Wishing for more coffee. Not wanting to see what everyone else seems to seek.

It's not his concern, but... isn't it weird? How can they *not* find the cave? Or... maybe they did and it was so insignificant that it wasn't mentioned? Yeah. That has to be it. 

Even if a *nagging* feeling tells him otherwise.

He takes a sip of his cup.
Only to be met with disappointment.

"Right." 

He's out of coffee.





it *is* pretty weird. Well, not *that* weird, people pretend to be kind all the time. Just wanting their face to be seen so that they can make a quick buck looking *charitable*. It's *sickening*. Let the idiots be idiots if they want. **He's** not going back at the very least. **Never**. He's never going back.



Taking a sip out of the most recent coffee mug, only to be met with disappointment, and the looming **threat** of sleep.

It would probably be good to take a walk.

"If you have any information-"

Get his mind of things.

"Please contact-"

TV off.

He- He doesn't need the reminders. The memories. He rather forget it all, yet a small lingering fear won't let it. The inkling that he'll be connected. The thought that the fool wrote something down, or took a picture while he wasn't looking. Something. Anything. That would connect the two. Part of him even wants to get closer to that sister who keeps showing her face as if she cares. Even though she's most likely just looking for attention, a way to find someone that could make her life easy. It makes sense, and, she's not fooling him. He's smarter than that.
