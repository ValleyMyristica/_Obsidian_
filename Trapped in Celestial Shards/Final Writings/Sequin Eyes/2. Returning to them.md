He's back.

He wakes.

Again and again.

He dreams of returning. A dream, so sweet and nice. A walk through the sparkling spring leaf's. A nightmare, a dance with death. A waltz in which one wrong move means a stab through the heart. Piercing and clear, an aching fear, with no escape. 

Trying to go through days as if it doesn't bother him.

Yet.

The bed terrifies him.
And the night brings fear.

Surrounded by empty coffee mugs, in a room he's sure to loose.

"We're still looking for the man."

Watching something stupid to get his mind of things.

"Jonathan-"

Is still *missing*.

"Even with the help of volunteers, there have been no new developments."

What? They haven't found that stupid cave yet?

"If you have any information that might help locate him. Please contact-"

Maybe it would be worth telling them about the location?

"It will be rewarded."

And, what is there to lose?

"Again, please contact-"

Yeah. Yeah.

Taking his phone, he gives a call. A simple call, telling of the cave, suggesting that the *random* man might have taken some shelter there. To which they thank him and promise to look into it.
