He's back.

He wakes.

Again and again.

He dreams of returning. A dream, so sweet and nice. A walk through the sparkling spring leaf's. A nightmare, a dance with death. A waltz in which one wrong move means a stab through the heart. Piercing and clear, an aching fear, **no escape**. 

Going through days as if it doesn't bother him.

Yet.

The bed terrifies.
And the night brings fear.

Surrounded by empty coffee mugs, in a room he's sure to loose.

"We're still looking for the man."

Watching something stupid to get his mind off things.

"My brother, Jonathan he-"

Is still *missing*.

Well. They haven't found the cave. *That terrifying thing*. A sight he sees so clear when eyes are closed. He keeps them open. Wishing for more coffee. Not wanting to see what everyone else seems to seek.

It's not his concern, but... isn't it weird? How can they *not* find the cave? Or... maybe they did and it was so insignificant that it wasn't mentioned? Yeah. That has to be it. 

Even if a *nagging* feeling tells him otherwise.

He takes a sip of his cup.
Only to be met with disappointment.

"Right." 

He's out of coffee.

Looking at the door to the outside.

He *should* go out. Get some air. Maybe get some new coffee too. Maybe he can blend into a workplace somewhere and get a free cup? Oh. How far he's fallen. He should be able to just go in, say his name and get everything for free! Don't they know who he is?!

Standing up, cup aside.

Coat on.

He's going to get that coffee.

Then. He sees his face in the mirror. 

Dirty. 

*A disappointment.*

"Shut up."

He leaves. 

*Who cares anyway.*

It's just the coffee shop.


Just go in, sit down, wait a little moment. *Eye the dirty dishes.* Hate how low you've fallen. Hate the thoughts in your head. Hate the fact you want to listen to them. Listen. 

Get up. Grab a dirty cup. Take a trip to the washroom and wash it as well as you can. Hate every moment of it. Curse all that led you to this point. Curse your father. He who could fix it all in the blink of an eye. 

Get out. Smile that fools allow refills. 'Refill' the cup with coffee. Sit down. Stare.

*Don't think about it.*

Drink.

Urgh.

Well, it's not *good* coffee, but it's at the very least better than... the dreams...

Feeling the warm porcelain of the white boring cup. Despite the caffeine, his body still feels heavy. Sitting down might not have been the best idea. Especially with the warm calming atmosphere. It's an awfully nice cafe. Probably put a lot of thought into it to try an manipulate costumers to stay and buy more stupid sweets. He's nothing like those idiots though, he won't fall for such simple tricks. HAH! He didn't even fall for the initial one.

Hah...

Tired.

Looking out the window. Watching the world. 
A world with so much potential.

He just needs to find his *in*.

Something that *clicks*.

Then.

Then everything will be good again.

"No. Haven't seen him." 

"I see... umm... could you take a paper at least, it would really help." She hands him a little paper.

He looks at it, then her, then out the window again, taking a sip of the coffee. **Cold.** Bitter and cold. *Nothing like the pretty snow, right?* He shakes the thought and turns to face her again.

She gives an awkward smile back.

To which he only glares back. Forces down the last of his disgusting bitter coffee and moves past her. Leaving the cup left on the table. He doesn't care about this. He just wants away. So, he *doesn't* listen at how she moves to bother other people.

He *just* walks outside. 

"I'm sorry miss-" He can hear, while not listening.

"Yes! Yes... I... I understand..." She responds, rustling with papers. "Could you at least put this up?"

"Miss..."

"Please?"

*Does she honestly think she'll be able to make a difference? What a fool. Just like her brother.* He thinks, discreetly listening in from where he stands, just by the side of the entrance door.

Walking out of the coffee shop. Another failure. Is asking around really that bad? Plus, what if those lonely people appreciated the company? She would have. Especially now...

Hugging the papers tighter.

Jo-jo was always so smart and full of life, always had a positive spin on everything. *How?* Looking up at the sky in longing for the past. 

A tap on the shoulder breaks her out.

"Yes!" She turns around, her face painted in tired excitement. Smiling as well as she can. "Oh." It's the tired guy from before. Does... does he want something? "Would... would you like a flier?" Maybe he changed his mind? He never really *did* answer.

"No."

"Oh." Like a sad little puppy, she lowers her head.

"Could you tell me about him instead?"

"Huh?" Facing the man in bewilderment. "I yeah- YEAH!" She's always happy to talk about her nice brother! So, she starts rambling.

"Cool." "Uh huh." "That's nice."

She keeps talking about all the nice memories.

"He even helped me." She tells.

"Did he now?"

"Yes, and we were meant to go and try and get me a job after his hike... but..."

"His hike? Did he say where he was going?"

"Not really?" 

He gives her a cold look.

"Well, he said he was trying to look for a cave."

"And, *why* wouldn't he do that in the summer?"

"I don't know? He didn't find it in the summer?"

"So he thought he'd find it in the winter?" A sigh. "Did you mention this?"

"Huh?"

"When you reported him missing. Did you say he was looking for some- cave?"

"Yes! Of course!"

"And?"

"There is no cave." That's what they all said.

"What?"

"He never really found it, so I guess that makes sense."

"What do you mean there's no cave? Yes there- is..."

"There is?" A glimmer of hope. "Have you seen it?"

"I-"

"Please!" Desperation.

"N- no."

"You have to tell me!" Taking hold of him with her hand, seeking all that he may know. "Have you seen my brother? Please?"

"I told you." Ripping himself free. "No." He tells stern and uncaring as she drops a few of her papers. 

"Right..." Downtrodden, slowly picking up her papers.

He leaves.

At home, opening the door, he makes sure to lock it behind him. Checking the handle just to be sure. Even makes sure that all the binds are down. He just- He can't have anyone watching.

It's a worthless restaurant job. A waiter, the lowest of the low. Well, at least he's not some cleaning lady. He'll get out of it though. He won't keep it for long. He just needs a little bit of the money. Then he'll quit and make it big. This time he'll succeed.

Looking at the *borrowed* clothing.

**Staring** at the clothing.

As if they could disappear if he looked away. He reaches for them, taking hold of the jacket. It's in his hand. It's solid. It's there. He brings it closer. And, *smells* it.
