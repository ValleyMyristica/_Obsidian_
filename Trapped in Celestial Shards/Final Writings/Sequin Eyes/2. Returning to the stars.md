*Welcome back.*

He turns. Ready to run. Yet, it seems he's walked far further than he'd like. Being meet with a multitude of corridors to run down.

He can hear the ice again. The scraping coming from all the halls. Surroundings distorted. Dry. It feels dry. It's autumn. Why is it dry? Leaning towards the wall. **Cold.** Moving away. He- he- he keeps moving. Looking towards the celling. It reminds him. Looking like the night sky, though, reminds him more of watchful eyes.

Arm hurts. The one that touched the wall. It **aches**.
