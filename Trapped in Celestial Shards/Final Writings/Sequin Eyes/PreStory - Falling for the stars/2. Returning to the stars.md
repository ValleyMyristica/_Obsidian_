*Welcome back.*

He turns. Ready to run. Yet, it seems he's walked far further than he'd like. Being meet with a multitude of corridors to run down.

He can hear the ice again. The scraping coming from all the halls. Surroundings distorted. Dry. It feels dry. It's autumn. Why is it dry? Leaning towards the wall. **Cold.** Moving away. He- he- he keeps moving. Looking towards the celling. It reminds him. Looking like the night sky, though, reminds him more of watchful eyes.

Arm hurts. The one that touched the wall. It **aches**. As if leaf's wants to spur up. Holding the arm. Grip tight. It aches. Like a slowly spreading cold. It feels like poison.

He runs.

Steps echo in the halls.

Halls he's seen far more than he'd like. 

It all feels like a play, and, he is simply to play his part. As if a pawn in someone else's game. Choices dwined and drown, disappearing with each step taken.

If only this was a dream to. 

How he wishes it was, even if his fear won't let him believe it is.

The light is in front.
The big hall is in view.

The place he swore to never come back to.

Yet. He *yearns*. No, not him. Something *in* him. As if his own body won't listen. As if there are strings attached. Movement not fully his.

*There is no choice.*

There is no choice. 

.

.

.


He stands in the hall.

He stands in silence.

Afraid of action.

He looks down. Water dripping down his neck. Holding his aching arm close. Like sweet poison, he can't let go. Breaths are short. Head feels light. In a way, he wishes to just faint. It's all a dream. It's all in his head. 

*Maybe he would have been better of dead?*

"No!" Lifting his head, stopping with dread

It echoes back. *No.*

He- What is he looking at? It's white. It's all so white. It looks like monsters. It looks like snow. He doesn't know. He doesn't know! He'd like to scream and run. Shout he's done. That he rather take death than play along.

Yet, *paralyzed*.

He can't move.

One of the white things approaches.

He can only watch.

Watch as it slowly gets closer, and stops. Watching him in turn, as if waiting.

"What do you want?" He tries.

It watches him, with eyes it does not possess. He simply feels exposed.

"Do you... want me to get you something?"

It takes a step closer. Sounding like the scraping of sand on ice.

"A- um- Sacrifice?" He wants to back away.

It gets closer, lifting what he'd best describe as an arm. He closes his eyes, expecting the worst. He can't run from this. It places its 'arm' on his head. **COLD**. Ice cold. So sure he'll end up dead. He can only hope it'll go quick.

A memory starts to surface. Telling to gather things. 

He'd appreciate it more if it was a memory of his part, of the days when everything was at his fingertips and he could get whatever he asked. When he had all. When he was happy.

Yet, most of what fills his mind. Is the **cold**. Making it hard to think. Hard to see. Hard to know what's reality. It's freezing. It's cold. It's pulsating. 

**It's gone.**

Everything. Empty.

Movements feel so mechanical as he stands up.

He *knows* what he should do.

Slow steps forward. Like a puppet on strings. It feels as a mockery. If it wasn't for the creeping silence, he'd think there was laughter echoing though the empty halls. Yet, there is nothing he can do but play his part.
