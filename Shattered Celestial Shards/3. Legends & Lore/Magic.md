> The distribution of magic

The sun gives magic to plants
The moon gives magic to animals
The stars give magic to (else)

## Plants
There are normal plants, and there are magical plants
There are different degrees of magic that can be found

* Little magic
Some with very little magic use the magic more just as a way to strengthen
But, apart from that it's just a normal plant

* Decent magic
Some with decent magic might be able to slowly more by shifting roots and deciding where to grow with a better understanding of the surrounding
These ones *look* like normal plants, but if you observe them you can take note of some "odd" behaviors

* Good magic
The ones with good magic can change their shape freely, though oftentimes there will be signs to show what sort of plant it was originally. These plants often use magic to help consume things that a plant might not be able to consume normally. 
-# (Not only can they transform themself, if they have a good understanding of they can also transform one thing into another)

* Great magic
Can transform quite freely and use magic in many different ways.

* Amazing!
Saffron and other sun children are almost more magic than plant, so they can use magic very freely.



> Plants in the world

There are a few "pure" plants in the world, but most plants hold a little magic and some hold a decent amount. It's more rare to find plants with good magic and the ones with great magic are very rare and mostly only find in places where a sun child is known to have lived.

For, the magic is given from the sun and it's only the sun child that is able to give magic more directly.

Fun fact:
Saffron's parents hold great magic



## Animals
Similar to plants, though there is a bigger distribution of "pure" animals. As animals mostly get their magic from eating enough magical plants to accumulate magic. Else, the magic that enters their system is mostly treated as just a nice boost of energy, nothing more.

Also,
Magic is full of nutriants, but some have a hard time consuming a lot of it. So, the plants that most animals enjoy eating are the ones with no, little  or decent magic. Else it'll be like a yest growing in you making you feel a bit bloated. (So be a bit careful when you eat plants with decent magic)


Other ways for animals to get magic was to be given it by the moon, but the moon does not have a lot of magic (just whatever it gets from the sun)

So, the only ones who ever gets magic from the moon is the moon's child
And the moon supports the child as much as possible, watching over as they grow


So, there *are* some animals with great magic, but they're more like legends and myths. And there are some with great magic, those are ones that people somewhat know exist, but most have not seen them. These animals often live in places with a lot of "magical plants" and magical plants enjoy to stay away from busy places.
