## [[3. Legends/The sun|The sun]]

When the first "plant" came to be, the sun was so very happy.
For a little thing that was so dependent on the sun to survive?
It was all one could ask

The sun cried a tear of joy at the sight
The tear fell onto the desolate ground
Making the soil a perfect place to grow

And as time passed on by
"Plants" started to spread
And they really seemed to like the spot where the "tear" had fallen.

So, the plants set their roots in the most precious of spots
And always looked up at the sun with a smile.
For in the suns patch of perfection, no flower will ever wither

Those happy smiles
It gave the sun such joy
Another tear was shed

This one, far more special
A little bit of the sun itself
Wanting to be closer
Wanting to be near

Starting as a seed
It took a long time till it fully grew
It was different from the others
More *conscious*







And then
A little animal came by

The plants couldn't run
The sun could only watch

As hatred grew
Another tear fell
Revenge.

It took so long for the tear to spark a bloom
The sun needed time
Needed perfection

The next one
A poison

And the animals fell
They learned
Never to come back




## [[3. Legends/The moon|The moon]]
There were never two moons
The moon was simply always watching the water
And, the moon always send a bit of magic down into the sea
Just to feel it, move the waves back and forth
Wanting to see a bit more than yesterday

The magic would sometimes hit peculiar creatures in the water
Never really knew what happened to those hit

Then a tardigrade got hit
And it... responded?

The moon kept trying to reach out to the tardigrade 
Wanting some company in the night
A friend

The tardigrade grew thanks to the magic given
Slowly becoming a "dragon"

The dragon lays in the sea
Immortal from the magic

The dragon is the only "child" of the moon
The original, the one the moon loves the most

> Told the sun, who told the moon
> The goal wasn't clear
> Just, wanting a change
