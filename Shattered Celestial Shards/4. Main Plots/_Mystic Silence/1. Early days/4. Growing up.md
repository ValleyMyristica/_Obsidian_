# \[ Ch.1 ] Meeting Sequin

Following mama like a little kitten. 
For mama will always keep them safe. 
Mama is best mama. Mama knows all the things. 

*'Mama only wants the best for me.'* 

Smiling softly, holding mama's hand as the two walk in silence. It feels a bit scary. Meeting new people is pretty scary. This sounded important too, but mama said that everything will be good.

"I love you, mama." Grip tightening.

"I love you too, sweetie." 

Mama stops, bending down and smiling at dear *Sweetie*.

"I know this feels big, but the leader will be there." She gives a little pat. "I heard he's also special, just like you."

Sweetie tilts her head.

"People say they can talk with the stars."

Wing ears perk.

"Maybe you two can become friends?" Mama smiles.

A nod. Feeling more confident.

"I love you so much." Standing up again. "Shall we continue?"

A little jump and a assured nod, taking mama's hand and ready to continue on. Always so happy to see mama happy. Mama is best mama. *I love mama.*

Mama really seemed to like the talk. She seems so energetic now, saying she'll do even better. But mama is already amazing. Sweetie tries to show its true, giving many hugs and some pats cause that's what you're supposed to do. Pats are really nice. Sweetie likes pats. 

Sweetie likes when mama smiles.

Mama turns towards sweetie with a smile. Eagerly picking them up. Muttering something hard to make out, but it sounds positive?

"Well, won't you tell your story." The leader asks softly floating by their side on the stage.

*Oh.* Sweetie looks up at mama. Unsure what mama's expression is saying. Sweetie gives mama a hug. Mama deserves the best hugs. Mama is the best. 

Mama starts to talk.

She seems nervus.
Hugging mama more.

Mama gives a pat, smiling, and talks more confidently.

When done.

The leader comes forth.

"A gift from the moon?" Interest peaked.

"Y-Yes!" She answers the leader. "My sweetie is the sweetest." Putting her hands on sweeties shoulders, guiding them to the leader. 

The leader looks down at them.

Sweetie looks up. The leader. The leader looks as if he's shining like a star. Even though it's the middle of the day. And... the skin sparkle like crystals. *Weird.* Holding onto mama's leg. The leader looks so... like an angle? White skin, glowing, with pretty wings attached to the hip, hovering. Able to be so imposing. 

"Come on sweetie." Mama says. "Say hi."

A little wave. Wanting to hide.

"Not much for words, are you?"

A shake.

"No matter." Smiling.

Turning towards the crowd, the leader lands, approaching the crowds with arms open and a well practiced commanding voice.

"*something something* ...for it seems the stars has helped the child of the moon find us."

The child tilts their head.

"Rejoice!"

Everyone cheers.

*Loud.* Sweetie clutches onto mama's leg. Holding onto her. Looking up, wanting to leave. Wanting to go home to that safety. Mama looks back with a smile, and gives a pat.

Pat's are nice.
