It's a pretty night. Sitting at the cold beach enjoying the sight of the half moon shimmering as the waves eb and flow softly by the shore. 

"I hurt you." He's holding them close, doing his best to keep them warm *with the heart they gave him*.

"It's okay." They like listening to his heartbeat. 

'*It's not.*' Is all his mind seems to supplement as he sits in silence with them. 

"I've been hurt a lot..." Burrowing closer into him.

"I know..." Holding them dear.

The waves are pretty and soft. It's nice, sitting down and listening to the time pass. Listening to the nights soft whispers.
