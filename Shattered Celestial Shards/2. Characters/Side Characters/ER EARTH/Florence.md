# Recipe
A wish has taken form
Words spoken loud
Weaving a pretty sight
Taking the magic
Dimming the light

A core is in hand
Which gives form
To the lifeless sand

A form white as snow,
Obsidian center
With gems of blue
And bloom of flame
The lifeless form is given a name

**Gratia.**

Here for you aid
Here to assist
Here. With gratitude.
