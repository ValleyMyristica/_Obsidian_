Name: Wither
(Fem Nonbinary, She/They) ~ [[Wither Dragon]]/[[Rose Dragon]] hybrid.

Wither is technically only half [[Wither Dragon]]? Her mother is a [[Rose Dragon]], but she takes more after her dad, and has all Wither Dragon abilities.

Wither is a princess, but not the heir, but is practically treated as such due to her strange biology. Her sister, [[Golden]], who is a pure [[Rose Dragon]] (Gold Rose Dragon, but you DON’T want to hear me rant about subspecies) and Golden tries to murder her one night. This fails, but it sends Wither out of the palace and into the lands of [[Rhynal]].
