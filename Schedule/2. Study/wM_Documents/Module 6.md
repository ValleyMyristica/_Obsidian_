> 'I spent my afternoons in a city park, pretending to read \_\_\_.' 
> From this quote, continue. Make the city park come alive through the use of verbs, e.g what are the trees doing? the lake? The ducks? the grass? etc. Delete the first, borrowed sentence before submitting your text.

The shimmering sunlight danced on the page, as it shone through the leaf's of the tree that was my shade. Distracting. *Wouldn't it be nice to dance along?* Feeling the paper page, unable to catch the light as it moves to my hand. 

Laughter is filling the park as children play. 
The winds are soft, like a warm embrace. 

It all feels so **safe**.

Right... *safe...*

Playing with the fleeting sunlight, remembering a park they once knew.

That one was safe too.

The sunlight fades, it's cloudy.

Remembering the past is something they rather not do.

They want to disconnect.

Looking down from the book, seeing the grass, so green it almost glows in health. Looking closer, small little critters walk about. So small, yet fighting to be alive. They could so easily be crushed and killed by someone why doesn't even know they exist. Still, they fight to be alive.

*'Disgusting bug.'* Echo in mind, words spoken in disgust from eyes better left forgotten.

"What's so wrong with being a bug?" Smiling as the little critters crawl away; Trying to shake the cold that creep up like vines trying to borrow into skin.

Looking away, up. Towards the lake, the centrepiece of the park. A waterjet spurts out from the middle, keeping the water alive and moving. It sounds like the rush of a waterfall, white and crashing down, before slowly turning into soft waves that hits the lakes edge. In a few corners, there are water lilies, the pretty flowers are in bloom.

The clouds pass.

And, in the sunlight, they shine in whites and soft pinks. 
The pretty colours, *reminiscent of a monster.*

It's as if they enjoy basking in the suns light.
Smiling softly, *of course they do.*
*The sun is kind to you.*

A sigh.
Hands shake.

*Could never be me.*
Looking up from under the tree.

The sun has not always been kind.
But, the park has always been safe.



"Context":
*How would a sunlit park feel if you feel hated by the sun?*
Our POV character is not one who's life has been kind, having met someone representing the sun who was far too cruel. It all felt like a dream, a nightmare more apt. Though, in that nightmare, the park was always safe, it was the only place of comfort. 

Loving the sun
But hating it too

They're just a little bug
*What can they do?*
