## Writing
Soft light from dim lamps as the outside is dull with the patter of rain. A cosy warm inside with a cup of hot chocolate in hand. 

So much chatter around, some talking about what they've made showing the screen. Others talking in a language one can't comprehend. Others stay in silence.


While two voices stand out.
They seem like friends, happy to talk about whatever.

Currently, the subject seems to be different rents, and how some stuff in small apartments don't really fit a lot of things.

"Can't have a sofa." One notes, as the conversation continues.

"A height-adjustable desk would be nice." The other tells, seemingly imagining where it would fit.

"Yeah! Actually. My dad's work place is moving.
So everything in storage was going to get thrown out."

"Wow."

"So, they said *go take whatever you'd like* even if it wasn't fully allowed."

"No one will know." 

"Exact, and in there my dad found an height-adjustable desk."

"Banger! Lucky. Imagen just *getting* one."

"Yeah, he hasn't put it up yet, but you know."

"Yeah, I get that, heavy stuff."

And just as quickly at that topic came, another takes it place.



## Reflection
It was quite hard to focus with all the sounds around. There were so many talking that it made it a challenge to just listen in to *one* conversation.

The conversations always go so fast, you try to listen, understand, gather and recall, but memory is such a fickle thing, and conversations always pass so fast. Right as you decide it's the one you want to use, it slowly starts to slip through your fingers like fine sand on the beach. 

Do hope one was able to build something proper
Even if every sandcastle is doomed to fall
