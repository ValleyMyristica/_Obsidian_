On thing of note that I think you *can* change is the odd inconsistency of saying she "had" given it, but then the speaker says they "took" it. Making it hard to fully connect that what the speaker is taking is what they've been given.

To clarify, I'd recommend you'd say something that implies that she is *currently* in the process of giving. Which helps connect it with the speaker being in the process of taking.

Or, you could have it the other way around, where she has indeed given it already, and the speaker notes that they take a sip of the glass bottle they've been given.

The important part isn't really if it's already been given or is in the process, the important part is that it's consistent though the perspective of both characters. Else you risk causing confusing the reader. 

(It took me a moment to fully grasp it was the same drink, I thought the speaker was drinking their water as a way to show "nah, I'm not starving myself, don't worry", while *only* consuming water)

Apart from that minor detail, I can't find any faults, it's very well written and you've done good in creating a nice atmosphere.

It does interest me that you chose to say "Her foot *slipped*," implying that she didn't fully mean to fall. Which makes one wonder, *why was she seemingly dancing on the railing?* If the word was "dropped", then it would sound like a more intentional act. But, that is not the case here, so... one does wonder... *what was her state of mind?*

And, considering the speaker was right there, did they know?
Why were they there in the first place?
Did she *want* them to see her fall?
Though, that doesn't sound like her at all

In a sense, saying "slipped" make it feel as though her state of mind was like a fog and she didn't have a full grasp of what she was doing. It feels sad and melancholic, like she's truly been damage and no one can see. Not even she can see it fully. But, maybe for a moment, that one moment where she "slipped", she came back to reality, only to watch her life flash by in the blink of an eye?

While "dropped" would be more concrete, like a choice. She might still not be in the right headspace, but she in a sense made a choice. Though, this word also leaves the door open to seeing that it could be an accident. Her taking a step, still lost in her mind fog, not even registering that there is nothing solid underneath, until she paints it with all the pain she held inside.

You could also use words like "leapt", to imply she very much did it with intent, maybe she wanted the speaker to see? Maybe she wanted them to save her? But, had already given up hope, and wanted to make a decision on her own.

## A curious cat

As the sky slowly shimmers in the colours reminiscent of her hair, a purple slowly turning dark blue. The night is falling, and she has a curiosity she must clench. A smile spreads as she stands at the foot of the big, abandoned house with a remote in hand.

Opening the ragged door, dust spreads, the remote beeps and she choughs, trying to look around. Stretching out the remote, she follows the beeps, the more, the better. She'll find that ghost. Moving further in, starting to realise that a dust mask could have be good to have. Though, it won't stop her, the remote is beeping more and more. She's getting closer.

Up the stairs, through a dilapidated corridor. The beeps lead her forward while she avoids any unstable looking planks. 

Then, a wall. 

Shoot.

Who could have predicted such a predicament?

Regardless, she's not one to give up that easily. She's like a cat in the night, wanting to explore and learn, and **catch** something new.

She touches the wall, wondering what secrets it might hold. She gives it a knock. Huh. She knocks again, taking note of the hollow sound, then tries to knock on the same wall a few paces away.

It's different!

She perks up, running back to the spot in which she was stopped. 

There must be a secret hidden.

She **will** find it.

Feeling the wall, pressing down on anything that feels *odd*, nothing. Taking a step back, she looks around. What's missing? Is she not seeing the full picture?

A dark candle sconce is attached to the wall.

Hmm...

It brings forth a childish thought, but... *why not?*

She pulls at it.

It breaks.

She falls.

She hits the floor.

She falls.

She hits... something *sloshy*.

The fall was dampened, and she can move without pain, but... she doesn't dare look down. Eyes fixated on the hole above. Hands feel *wet*. Whatever sha landed in, it feels *organic* and *fresh*. It's not a ghost, it's too corporeal for that.

Slowly trying to move away from *the pile*.

Everything moves like balls in a ball pit submerged in slime. 

Finally, she reaches the end, the end of the apparent box she's found herself in. Still looking up; she climbs out. Only to hear the echoing of steps approach. Her remote starts to beep, where it's submerged in the pile. Directing her eyes down before she can stop herself.

**Freeze.**

Her body stills as a burning acid starts to fill her thought and a cold shiver claws it way up her spine. Jaw shakes. Eyes dart around. As two thoughts fill her mind.

*'I need to get out.'*

And.

*'Curiosity killed the cat.'*
